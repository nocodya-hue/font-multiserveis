About us

Your project, our commitment.

Font Multiserveis is a renovation and home-services company based in Muro, in the north of Mallorca. We are a team of professionals with more than 10 years of experience in construction and painting.

The company is run by its manager, Toni Font. We work for homeowners, residents’ associations and businesses, and we also look after holiday rentals before and after the season.
